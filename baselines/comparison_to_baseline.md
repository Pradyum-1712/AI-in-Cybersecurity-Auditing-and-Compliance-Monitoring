## Extensions and Gaps

### Our Contributions
1. **Extended Coverage:** Our survey includes 40 papers specifically focused on AI in auditing and compliance monitoring
2. **Temporal Analysis:** We analyze trends from 2022-2025, providing recent insights
3. **Comparative Framework:** Developed evaluation criteria for comparing different approaches

### Gaps Identified in Baseline
1. Limited focus on compliance monitoring applications
2. Fewer real-world implementation case studies
3. Less emphasis on automated auditing workflows

### Novel Aspects of Our Survey
- Comprehensive taxonomy of AI techniques vs. auditing stages
- Performance comparison across different methodologies
- Research gap identification for future work
